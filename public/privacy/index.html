Privacy

Your practice log belongs to you. The core product has no account, analytics, advertising, or employer view.

Data stored on your device

Practice blocks, prompts, questions, and work-use notes stay in this browser’s IndexedDB database.

Theme and license state stay in localStorage. Normal logging does not send this information away.

Clearing site data removes it, so export JSON if you need a backup.

What leaves your device

Logging, review, import, and export use no network requests for record content.

A purchase opens Sociobot’s hosted checkout. A license check sends only the license token to Sociobot’s API.

The API never receives your log content.

Payments

Sociobot and Dodo process the one-time purchase as merchant of record. This app never receives or stores card details.

Offline cache

A service worker stores the app shell and illustration after your first visit. This lets the log reload without a network connection.

Clear the site’s browser storage to remove those files.

Workplace privacy

Do not enter customer names, credentials, private URLs, incident identifiers, proprietary code, or other confidential employer information.

Describe general patterns and what you noticed instead.

Your choices

You can export all records as JSON or CSV. You can import a backup, delete records, or clear browser data.

No accessibility, export, or safety feature requires payment.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
