Privacy

Your practice log belongs to you. The core product has no account, analytics, advertising, or employer view.

Data stored on your device

Practice blocks, retrieval prompts, open questions, and work-use notes are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB database. Theme and license state are stored in localStorage. This information is not sent to us. Clearing site data removes it from that device, so export a JSON backup if you need one.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device during normal logging, review, import, or export. If you buy or restore the optional Evidence Pass, your browser opens Sociobot’s hosted checkout or sends the license token to Sociobot’s API to check whether it is valid. The log contents are never included in that request.

Payments

Sociobot and Dodo act as merchant of record for the one-time purchase and process payment information under their own notices. This app does not receive or store card details.

Offline cache

A service worker keeps the application shell and original illustration on your device so the log can open without a network connection. You can remove these files by clearing site storage.

Workplace privacy

Do not enter customer names, credentials, private URLs, incident identifiers, proprietary code, or other confidential employer information. Describe general patterns and what you noticed instead.

Your choices

You can export all records as JSON or CSV, import a JSON backup, delete individual records, or clear the site’s browser data. No accessibility, export, or safety feature requires payment.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
